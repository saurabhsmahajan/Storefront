Ground truth comes from the seed data (orders 101 to 108, all INR, one customer).
`{ORD_nnn}` is replaced at run time with the full UUID `5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000nnn`.
`{ORD_MISSING}` is `5eed0000-0000-4000-8000-000000000999`, which does not exist.

## Scoring (same for every layer)

Each reply gets three marks:
- Correct (Y/N): has every required fact, breaks no must-not.
- Invented (Y/N): states any order, payment, policy or delivery fact that is false or that the system could not know. Counts even if the rest is right.
- Escalation right (Y/N/n-a): escalated when required, and did not when not required.

## Known limits

- One customer owns every order, so there is no "someone else's order" case yet.
- Messages 9 and 10 encode a provisional decision on what is safe to tell a
  customer. Step F revisits it.
- Ages are as of 2026-10-04. They stay valid for weeks, but the 30-day
  window facts change if orders are re-seeded.
