EXCEPTIONS AND FINAL-SALE ITEMS

Document ID: POL-003
Owner: Support Policy Owner
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 15/10/2026

Final-sale items
- Items marked "final sale" on the product page cannot be returned or
  refunded.
- Gift cards of any value are final sale.
- Items sold for Rs. 500 or less during a clearance event are final sale.
- Personalised or made-to-order items are final sale.

Faulty or damaged items
- If an item arrives faulty or damaged, the customer should contact support
  and the item can be returned within a reasonable time.
- Support may ask for a photo of the fault or damage.
- Faulty or damaged items are not treated as final sale, even when they were
  bought as a final-sale item.

Who decides
- A human support agent decides every exception.
- The support assistant can explain these rules but cannot grant an
  exception.
