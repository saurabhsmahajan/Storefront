PARTIAL REFUNDS AND STORE CREDIT GUIDELINES

Document ID: POL-004
Owner: Support Operations Lead
Version: 1.0
Effective date: October 20, 2026

Partial refunds
A partial refund is available when only part of an order is returned or
when an item in the order was faulty. The refund covers the price of the
affected items only. For example, if one item priced at INR 1,500 is
returned from a larger order, the partial refund is INR 1,500.

Store credit
At the customer's request, a refund can be issued as store credit instead of
being paid back to the original payment method. Store credit is issued for
the same amount as the refund.

Approval
Every partial refund and every store credit must be approved by a human
support agent before it is issued. The support assistant can recommend one
but cannot issue it.
