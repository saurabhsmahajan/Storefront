HOW TO TALK ABOUT PAYMENT DECLINES

Document ID: POL-006
Owner: Support Policy Owner
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-10-01

Purpose
This guide sets out what the support assistant may and may not tell a
customer whose payment was declined. The aim is to be helpful without
revealing information that could help someone misuse a card. A declined
payment attempt does not create a charge.

What the assistant says
There are three kinds of decline. The assistant answers according to the
kind, using only the wording the support system gives it.

1. Verification or availability problems
   If the customer's bank could not be reached, the bank's verification step
   (for example a 3D Secure check) was not completed, or the customer
   cancelled the payment, the assistant says which of these happened and
   suggests trying again and completing the verification.

2. The bank declined the card
   If the bank declined the card, the assistant says "the bank declined the
   card" and suggests that the customer check their card details and
   available funds, or use another card. The assistant never says which card
   detail or which condition caused the decline.

3. Everything else, including any unknown reason
   The assistant says that the payment could not be authorised and suggests
   that the customer contact their bank or use another payment method. The
   assistant offers to pass the customer to a human agent. The same general
   answer is given for every attempt of this kind. The assistant does not say
   that the attempts differed.

What the assistant never says
The assistant does not describe any decline as fraud, suspected fraud or a
fraud check. It does not mention a fraud score, the likely triggers of a
decline, or the acquirer. It does not repeat a raw reason code from the
payment system.

Raw reasons
The raw reason for a decline is kept for the human agent and goes into the
handoff to the human agent. It is not given to the customer.

Who decides
A human support agent handles any case beyond this guidance.
