STOREFRONT REFUND ELIGIBILITY POLICY

Document ID: POL-001
Owner: Support Policy Owner
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-10-01
Replaces: refund and return policy v0 (Week 2 test version)

1. Eligibility window
   A paid order can be refunded within 30 days of the order date.
   After 30 days an order is not eligible for a refund.

2. Orders that cannot be refunded
   2.1 Orders that have already been refunded.
   2.2 Orders that were never paid (status pending). Nothing was charged, so
       there is nothing to refund. A failed payment attempt does not create
       a charge.

3. Refund amount
   A refund is for the full order total. Partial refunds are not offered.

4. How refunds are paid
   Refunds go back to the original payment method. They are not issued as
   store credit.

5. Approval
   Every refund must be approved by a human support agent before it is
   issued. The support assistant can check eligibility and recommend a
   refund, but it cannot issue one.

6. Disagreeing with a decision
   Customers who disagree with a refund decision can ask for a human agent
   at any time.
