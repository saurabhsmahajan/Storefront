DISPUTE AND CHARGEBACK HANDLING

Document ID: POL-005
Owner: Payments Operations Lead
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 01-Nov-2026

A dispute is raised when a customer questions a charge. A chargeback is a
dispute that the customer has taken to their bank or card issuer.

Handling rules
- A human support agent handles every dispute and every chargeback. The
  support assistant can explain the process but cannot respond to a bank.
- An order with an open chargeback must not also be refunded. This avoids
  paying the customer twice.
- Support gathers the order record, the payment record and the delivery
  record before responding to the bank.
- The assistant must not describe a chargeback as a fraud finding.

Stages and targets

| Stage                  | Handled by      | Target (days) | Review threshold      |
| ---------------------- | --------------- | ------------- | --------------------- |
| Dispute received       | Support agent   | 2             | Over EUR 250          |
| Evidence gathered      | Support agent   | 5             | Over USD 300          |
| Response sent to bank  | Payments team   | 7             | Over EUR 250          |
| Outcome recorded       | Payments team   | 2             | Not applicable        |

The review threshold is the order amount above which a senior agent must
review the case before the response is sent.
